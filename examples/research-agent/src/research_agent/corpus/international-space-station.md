# The International Space Station

The International Space Station (ISS) is a joint project of the United States, Russia, Europe, Japan and Canada. Its first module, Zarya, launched on November 20, 1998, and the US Unity node followed in December 1998.

The first resident crew, Expedition 1, arrived on November 2, 2000, and the station has been continuously occupied ever since. The ISS orbits at an altitude of about 400 kilometers, circling the Earth roughly every 92 minutes.

With a mass of about 420,000 kilograms, it is the largest structure humans have assembled in space. It has hosted crews from more than 20 countries.
