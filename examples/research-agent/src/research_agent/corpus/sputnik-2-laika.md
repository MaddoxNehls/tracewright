# Sputnik 2 and Laika

Sputnik 2 launched on November 3, 1957, less than a month after Sputnik 1. It carried Laika, a stray dog from the streets of Moscow, who became the first animal to orbit the Earth.

The mission was assembled in a rush to coincide with the anniversary of the October Revolution, and there was no way to return Laika to Earth. She died within hours of launch from overheating when the cabin's thermal control failed, a fact not publicly confirmed until 2002.

Despite its tragic outcome, Sputnik 2 showed that a living creature could survive launch and enter weightlessness, data that informed the design of crewed spacecraft.
