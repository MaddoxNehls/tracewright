# Project Gemini

Project Gemini was NASA's bridge between Mercury and Apollo. Its two-person spacecraft flew ten crewed missions in 1965 and 1966 to practice the skills a Moon landing would need.

Gemini 7 stayed in orbit for nearly 14 days in December 1965, proving that humans could endure the length of a lunar mission. On December 15, 1965, Gemini 6A and Gemini 7 performed the first crewed space rendezvous, approaching within 30 centimeters of each other.

On March 16, 1966, Neil Armstrong and David Scott in Gemini 8 performed the first docking of two spacecraft, with an uncrewed Agena target vehicle. A stuck thruster then sent them into a dangerous spin, and Armstrong's quick recovery helped earn him later command of Apollo 11.
