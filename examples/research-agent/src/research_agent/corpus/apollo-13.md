# Apollo 13

Apollo 13 launched on April 11, 1970, to be the third Moon landing, with Jim Lovell, Jack Swigert and Fred Haise.

About 56 hours into the flight, on April 13, an oxygen tank in the service module exploded, crippling the command module's power and life support. The landing was abandoned. The crew used the lunar module Aquarius as a lifeboat, and engineers on the ground improvised ways to stretch its consumables, including an adapter for the command module's square carbon dioxide scrubbers.

Apollo 13 swung around the Moon and splashed down safely on April 17, 1970. The flight is remembered as a "successful failure" and for the phrase "Houston, we've had a problem."
