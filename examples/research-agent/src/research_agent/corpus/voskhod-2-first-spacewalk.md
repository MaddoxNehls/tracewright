# Voskhod 2 and the First Spacewalk

On March 18, 1965, cosmonaut Alexei Leonov performed the first extravehicular activity (spacewalk), leaving the Voskhod 2 spacecraft through an inflatable airlock for about 12 minutes.

His spacesuit ballooned in the vacuum, stiffening so badly that he could not re-enter the airlock. Leonov secretly bled air from the suit, risking decompression sickness, until he could squeeze back inside. The mission's troubles continued: the automatic landing system failed and the crew landed far off course in the snowy forests of the Ural Mountains.

The first American spacewalk followed less than three months later, when Ed White left Gemini 4 on June 3, 1965 for about 23 minutes.
