# The Hubble Space Telescope

The Hubble Space Telescope was launched on April 24, 1990, aboard Space Shuttle Discovery on mission STS-31. It orbits above the distorting effects of Earth's atmosphere.

Soon after launch, astronomers discovered that its 2.4-meter primary mirror had been ground to the wrong shape, blurring its images. In December 1993, the STS-61 servicing mission installed corrective optics. Astronauts serviced Hubble five times in total, the last in 2009.

Hubble's observations helped pin down the age of the universe at about 13.8 billion years and contributed to the discovery that the expansion of the universe is accelerating.
