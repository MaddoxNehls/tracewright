# Project Mercury

Project Mercury was the first American human spaceflight program, running from 1958 to 1963 with seven selected astronauts known as the Mercury Seven.

On May 5, 1961, Alan Shepard flew Freedom 7 on a suborbital flight lasting 15 minutes and 22 seconds, becoming the first American in space. Unlike Gagarin, Shepard had some manual control of his capsule's attitude.

On February 20, 1962, John Glenn became the first American to orbit the Earth, circling it three times in Friendship 7 during a flight of about 4 hours and 55 minutes. Mercury flew six crewed missions in total, ending with Gordon Cooper's 22-orbit Faith 7 flight in May 1963.
