# Sputnik 1

Sputnik 1 was the first artificial Earth satellite. The Soviet Union launched it on October 4, 1957, from the Baikonur Cosmodrome aboard an R-7 rocket, the world's first intercontinental ballistic missile.

The satellite was a polished metal sphere 58 centimeters in diameter with four external radio antennas, and it weighed 83.6 kilograms. It orbited the Earth roughly every 96 minutes and broadcast a simple radio beep that amateur operators around the world could hear.

Its batteries ran out after 21 days, and Sputnik 1 burned up on reentry on January 4, 1958. The launch shocked the United States, triggered the "Sputnik crisis", and marked the start of the Space Race.
