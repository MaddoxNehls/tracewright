# Salyut 1 and Skylab: The First Space Stations

The Soviet Union launched Salyut 1, the world's first space station, on April 19, 1971. Its first resident crew, aboard Soyuz 11, spent 23 days on board, but all three cosmonauts died on June 30, 1971, when their capsule depressurized during return. They remain the only people to have died in space.

NASA's first station, Skylab, launched on May 14, 1973, on the final Saturn V flight, built from a converted rocket stage. It was damaged during launch and repaired in orbit by its first crew. Three crews visited, the last staying 84 days.

Skylab reentered the atmosphere on July 11, 1979, scattering debris over Western Australia.
