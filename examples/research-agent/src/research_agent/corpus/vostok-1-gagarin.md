# Vostok 1: Yuri Gagarin

On April 12, 1961, Soviet cosmonaut Yuri Gagarin became the first human to travel into space and the first to orbit the Earth, aboard Vostok 1.

The flight lasted 108 minutes and completed a single orbit. The spacecraft was controlled automatically; the controls were locked, because no one knew how a person would function in weightlessness. Gagarin ejected from the capsule at about 7 kilometers altitude and landed separately by parachute.

His radio exclamation at liftoff, "Poyekhali!" ("Let's go!"), became famous. Gagarin died in a training jet crash in 1968. April 12 is celebrated as Cosmonautics Day in Russia.
