# Mir

Mir was a modular Soviet and later Russian space station. Its core module launched on February 20, 1986, and further modules were added over the following ten years.

Mir hosted the longest single spaceflight in history: physician Valeri Polyakov spent 437 days aboard in 1994 and 1995. Through the Shuttle-Mir program, Space Shuttles docked with the station nine times between 1995 and 1998, giving NASA long-duration experience before the ISS.

The station survived a fire and a collision with a cargo ship in 1997. Mir was deliberately deorbited into the Pacific Ocean on March 23, 2001.
