# The Saturn V Rocket

The Saturn V was the super heavy-lift rocket that carried astronauts to the Moon. Designed under Wernher von Braun at the Marshall Space Flight Center, it stood 110.6 meters (363 feet) tall and weighed about 2,900 tonnes when fueled.

Its first stage used five F-1 engines that together produced about 34.5 meganewtons (7.5 million pounds-force) of thrust at liftoff. The three-stage rocket could send roughly 43 tonnes toward the Moon.

The Saturn V flew 13 times between November 9, 1967 (the uncrewed Apollo 4) and May 14, 1973, when it launched the Skylab space station. It never lost a payload.
