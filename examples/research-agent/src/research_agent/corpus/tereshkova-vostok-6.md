# Valentina Tereshkova and Vostok 6

On June 16, 1963, Valentina Tereshkova became the first woman in space, launching aboard Vostok 6. She was a textile worker and amateur parachutist selected from more than 400 applicants.

Tereshkova orbited the Earth 48 times over nearly three days, longer than the combined flight time of every American astronaut up to that date. Her flight overlapped with Valery Bykovsky's Vostok 5.

It would be nearly two decades before another woman flew: Svetlana Savitskaya in 1982. Sally Ride became the first American woman in space in 1983.
