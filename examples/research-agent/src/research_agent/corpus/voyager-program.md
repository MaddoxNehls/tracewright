# The Voyager Probes

NASA launched two Voyager probes in 1977 to take advantage of a rare planetary alignment: Voyager 2 on August 20 and Voyager 1 on September 5.

Both flew past Jupiter and Saturn. Voyager 2 continued on to become the only spacecraft to visit Uranus (1986) and Neptune (1989). Each probe carries a Golden Record of sounds and images from Earth.

Voyager 1 crossed the heliopause into interstellar space on August 25, 2012, the first human-made object to do so, and Voyager 2 followed on November 5, 2018. Both are still transmitting, more than 20 billion kilometers from home.
