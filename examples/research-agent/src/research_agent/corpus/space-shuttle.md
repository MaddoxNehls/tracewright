# The Space Shuttle

The Space Shuttle was NASA's partially reusable crewed spacecraft. The first flight, STS-1, launched on April 12, 1981, exactly 20 years after Gagarin's flight, with John Young and Robert Crippen aboard Columbia.

Five orbiters flew in space: Columbia, Challenger, Discovery, Atlantis and Endeavour. The fleet flew 135 missions, deploying the Hubble Space Telescope and building much of the International Space Station.

Two disasters killed 14 astronauts. Challenger broke apart 73 seconds after launch on January 28, 1986, and Columbia disintegrated during reentry on February 1, 2003. The final mission, STS-135 flown by Atlantis, landed on July 21, 2011.
