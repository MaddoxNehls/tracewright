# The Apollo Program in Numbers

Between 1969 and 1972, NASA's Apollo program landed six crews on the Moon: Apollo 11, 12, 14, 15, 16 and 17. Apollo 13's landing was aborted after an in-flight explosion.

Twelve astronauts walked on the lunar surface. Together the six landing missions returned about 382 kilograms (842 pounds) of lunar rocks, core samples and soil to Earth. Apollo 15, 16 and 17 carried the Lunar Roving Vehicle, extending how far astronauts could explore.

The final mission, Apollo 17, launched in December 1972; Eugene Cernan was the last person to walk on the Moon. The program cost about 25.8 billion dollars at the time.
