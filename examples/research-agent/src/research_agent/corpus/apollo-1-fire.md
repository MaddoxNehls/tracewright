# The Apollo 1 Fire

On January 27, 1967, a fire broke out inside the Apollo command module during a launch rehearsal test at Cape Kennedy. Astronauts Gus Grissom, Ed White and Roger Chaffee were killed.

The cabin was pressurized with pure oxygen above sea-level pressure, and it held flammable materials such as nylon netting and Velcro. A spark, probably from damaged wiring, ignited a fire that spread in seconds. The inward-opening hatch could not be opened against the cabin pressure.

The investigation led to sweeping changes: a quick-opening outward hatch, a nitrogen-oxygen atmosphere on the launch pad, and fire-resistant materials. Crewed Apollo flights resumed with Apollo 7 in October 1968.
