# Commercial Crew and SpaceX Demo-2

After the Space Shuttle retired in 2011, the United States relied on Russian Soyuz spacecraft to send astronauts to the ISS. NASA's Commercial Crew Program paid private companies to develop new crewed spacecraft.

On May 30, 2020, SpaceX launched NASA astronauts Bob Behnken and Doug Hurley aboard the Crew Dragon spacecraft on a Falcon 9 rocket. The Demo-2 mission was the first crewed orbital launch from American soil since 2011 and the first by a private company.

The Falcon 9 had pioneered propulsive booster landings: its first stage first landed intact on December 21, 2015. Reusable boosters have since dramatically lowered launch costs.
