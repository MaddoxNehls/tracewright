# Apollo 8

Apollo 8 was the first crewed spacecraft to leave Earth orbit, reach the Moon, orbit it, and return. It launched on December 21, 1968, with Frank Borman, Jim Lovell and William Anders, and was the first crewed launch of the Saturn V.

The spacecraft entered lunar orbit on December 24 and circled the Moon ten times. During the fourth orbit, Anders photographed "Earthrise", the Earth rising over the lunar horizon, one of the most influential photographs ever taken. On Christmas Eve the crew read from the Book of Genesis in a broadcast watched by an estimated one billion people.

Apollo 8 splashed down in the Pacific on December 27, 1968.
