# Explorer 1

Explorer 1 was the first satellite of the United States. It launched on January 31, 1958 (local time) atop a Juno I rocket from Cape Canaveral, developed by the Army Ballistic Missile Agency team led by Wernher von Braun.

The satellite carried a cosmic-ray detector designed by physicist James Van Allen. Its readings revealed belts of charged particles trapped by Earth's magnetic field, now called the Van Allen radiation belts. This was the first major scientific discovery of the Space Age.

Explorer 1 weighed about 14 kilograms and transmitted data until May 1958. It remained in orbit until 1970.
