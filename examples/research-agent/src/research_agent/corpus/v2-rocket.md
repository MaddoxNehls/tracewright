# The V-2 Rocket

The V-2 (Aggregat 4) was a liquid-fueled ballistic missile developed in Nazi Germany under a team that included Wernher von Braun. It burned ethanol and liquid oxygen and was built largely with forced labor, which killed thousands of prisoners.

On June 20, 1944, a vertically launched V-2 test flight (MW 18014) reached an altitude of about 176 kilometers, making it the first human-made object to cross the Karman line into space.

After the war, both the United States and the Soviet Union captured V-2 hardware and engineers. Von Braun and many of his colleagues moved to the United States under Operation Paperclip and later designed the Redstone, Jupiter and Saturn rockets.
