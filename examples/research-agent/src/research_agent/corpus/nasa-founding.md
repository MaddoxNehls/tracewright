# The Founding of NASA

The National Aeronautics and Space Administration (NASA) was created in response to Sputnik. President Dwight D. Eisenhower signed the National Aeronautics and Space Act on July 29, 1958, and NASA began operations on October 1, 1958.

NASA absorbed the National Advisory Committee for Aeronautics (NACA), along with its roughly 8,000 employees and three research laboratories. It soon took over the Army's Jet Propulsion Laboratory and von Braun's rocket team in Huntsville, which became the Marshall Space Flight Center.

Making the agency civilian rather than military was a deliberate choice, signaling that American space exploration would be conducted openly.
